## Who I am

I'm Liam, known online as That Bearded IT Guy. I spent 13 years in the British Army before moving into IT, and I've been working in IT support for a Managed Service Provider since December 2022, across Microsoft 365, Intune, and Entra ID for a range of client environments.

The Army gave me something I didn't fully appreciate until I'd left it: a habit of trusting process over improvisation, and taking responsibility for the people and systems around you rather than just the task directly in front of you. Those two things shape how I approach IT more broadly, not just the day-to-day support work.

## Where I'm headed

The direction I'm building toward is IT service governance: problem management, service improvement, and IT governance analyst roles, rather than the conventional L1 to L2 to L3 technical progression. What draws me isn't the depth of a single technical fault, it's the pattern behind repeat problems, the process that stops them recurring, and the documentation that means nobody has to solve the same thing twice. I write more about that reasoning on the blog.

## Outside of work

Most of what doesn't go toward client tickets goes into my homelab: a Proxmox host running Docker containers and Plex, which has turned out to be a genuinely good low-stakes place to practice the same instincts I use professionally, backups, change control, documentation, without a client on the other end if I get something wrong. You can find the full write-ups on the Projects page.
